Frequently Asked Questions

What is PaisaPay?
PaisaPay is a rewards platform where you can earn real money by completing simple tasks, inviting friends, and participating in various earning opportunities. It's a legitimate way to make extra income online from your smartphone or computer.
How do I earn money on PaisaPay?
You can earn money through multiple ways:
  • Complete Tasks: Visit websites, watch videos, or perform simple actions.
  • Refer Friends: Share your referral code and earn ₹100 per friend.
  • Bonuses: Get rewarded for completing milestones and achievements.
  • Daily Rewards: Log in daily to earn bonus points.
How much can I earn?
Your earnings depend on your activity level:
  • Per Task: ₹5 - ₹50 per task
  • Per Referral: ₹100 per successful referral
  • Monthly Potential: Active users earn ₹1000 - ₹5000 monthly
  • No Limits: The more you engage, the more you earn!
How do I withdraw my earnings?
Withdrawing your earnings is simple:
  1. Go to the Withdraw page from the bottom navigation.
  2. Select your preferred payment method (UPI, Bank Transfer, etc.).
  3. Enter the amount you want to withdraw (minimum ₹50).
  4. Submit your request and wait for processing (24-48 hours).
What is the minimum withdrawal amount?
The minimum withdrawal amount is ₹50, and the maximum is ₹50,000 per request. Larger amounts may require additional verification.
How long do withdrawals take?
Withdrawals are typically processed within 24-48 hours. Some payment methods like UPI may be processed faster. You'll receive a notification when your withdrawal is complete. For any delays, please contact our support team.
How do I get my referral code?

Getting Started

1. Create Your Account

Download the PaisaPay app or visit our website. Sign up using your phone number and verify your account with OTP. Complete your profile by adding your name and email address.

2. Explore the Dashboard

The dashboard gives you an overview of your earnings, tasks, referrals, and account status. Familiarize yourself with the different sections:

  • Home: Quick overview of your stats and active tasks.
  • Earn: Browse and complete available tasks.
  • Invite: Share your referral code and track referrals.
  • Withdraw: Request withdrawals of your earnings.
  • Profile: Manage your personal information and settings.
3. Start Earning

Begin by completing simple tasks like visiting websites, watching videos, or performing quick actions. Each task has a clear reward amount shown on the Earn page.

4. Refer Friends

Share your referral code with friends and family. You earn ₹100 for each friend who signs up using your code and completes their first task. There's no limit to how many friends you can refer!

Pro Tip: Complete your profile and verify your account to unlock higher-paying tasks and faster withdrawals.

Withdrawal Guide

How to Withdraw
  1. Navigate to the Withdraw page from the bottom navigation.
  2. Select your preferred payment method (UPI, Bank Transfer, etc.).
  3. Enter the amount you wish to withdraw (minimum ₹50).
  4. Provide the required account details for your selected method.
  5. Click Request Withdrawal and confirm your request.
  6. Wait for processing (typically 24-48 hours).
Supported Payment Methods
  • UPI: Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, and other UPI apps.
  • Bank Transfer: Direct transfer to your bank account.
  • Cryptocurrency: USDT (TRC20) for crypto users.
  • Digital Wallets: Various digital wallet options.
Withdrawal Rules
  • Minimum Amount: ₹50 per withdrawal.
  • Processing Time: 24-48 hours for most methods.
  • Processing Fee: No processing fee for withdrawals.
  • Verification: Large withdrawals may require additional verification.
  • Limits: Maximum ₹50,000 per withdrawal request.
  • Tasks Required: You'll need to have completed at least 10 tasks since your last withdrawal.
  • Referrals Required: You'll need at least 5 verified referrals since your last withdrawal.

Tip: Double-check your account details before submitting a withdrawal request to avoid delays or failed transactions.
